# Frozen observed-feature feasibility protocol

Bind all eighty original MAIN classifier observations from the completed
class-local source checkpoint 388a9a1. The preceding source's additional eighty
archival assessments are not eighty more independent heads. Preserve all source
bytes and old numerical modules; freeze new source/settings/roles/seeds before
scientific generation or candidate scoring.

Main has ten archived observations per weak-linear, standard-linear,
allometry+saturation and confounded-size law at original reference N=64/256.
These are archived heads with fresh diagnostic cohorts, not fresh neural training.
Eight separate preflight observations reuse the first head in each cell with
independent roles and are excluded from performance estimates.

For each observation: detector training has known 1,024/nearby 512/distant 512;
independent calibration has the same sizes; external known IID has 2,048 and
nearby/distant/exact-overlap cohorts have 512 each. Confounded-size evaluation
also has 2,048 shifted-known specimens. No overlap/shifted examples enter
training or calibration. Four measured morphology and eight ecology traits are
the only detector features. Generator laws are unchanged.

One frozen gradient-boosting classifier uses 100 depth-three trees, log loss,
learning rate .1, minimum leaf ten, all rows/features, fixed random state 75101
and no early stopping. All remaining parameters are explicit in the configuration
module. The real experiment uses the source's exact numerical runtime. Native
float32 tree comparisons are independently reconstructed from numeric node arrays,
with every staged logit and training loss checked at rtol/atol 1e-12.

Use known_score >= j/100 for j=0..100. Independently select each head's threshold
on calibration data alone: known coverage >=80%, accepted known accuracy >=90%,
nearby AND distant acceptance <=10%. Maximize qualifying known coverage; choose
the larger threshold on ties. A valid lack of a qualifying point is a non-success
with its own status; numerical unavailability is separately bounded. Preserve
all calibration/evaluation curves, including undefined accepted accuracies.

New seed domain [20261112,750,...] identifies phase, law, original N, replicate
and role before the unchanged cohort/innovation suffixes. The frozen registry
has 902 cohorts, 720,896 rows and 4,510 unique innovation paths across phases.
Main: 820 cohorts/655,360 rows; preflight: 82 cohorts/65,536. No replay adds data.

Main: eighty detector fits plus four exact fit replays and eight original frozen
prediction/policy replay workflows: 92 work units, 84 detector fits/8,400 trees.

workflows: twelve units, ten detector fits/1,000 trees. Total 94 binary fits and
9,400 trees. No neural/logistic retraining, GMM fits, null/significance draws,
stability resampling, empirical fitting or author contact. Engineering fixtures
and regression tests are separately identified.

Retain all three neural seeds; logistic and residual zero are primary. Sixty-four
primary external joint-target rates span eight cells, two primary heads and
diagnostic/probability/distance/local screens. Give exact marginal and separate
Bonferroni-64 simultaneous intervals, requested denominators and failure bounds.
Forty-eight paired contrasts retain discordances and continuous changes. Original
unscreened outputs and per-class, overlap and shift outcomes remain descriptive.
Datasets are independent units; policies, seeds, thresholds and rows repeat them.
Ten source observations/cell support a pilot with wide uncertainty.

Prespecify one descriptive stopping flag, combined measurements at N=256 with
residual zero: at least 8/10 qualify on calibration; at least 5/10 meet the original
external joint target; at least three diagnostic-only successes versus class-local
support and at most one reverse success. Other cells cannot replace this focal
cell post hoc. This flag allocates research effort; it is no significance test,
formal attainability bound or deployment guarantee. Extra data and unfamiliar
labels prevent attribution of any gain solely to an architecture.

If the flag passes, document the favourable assumptions and precise gap; further
deployable work requires a separate proposal. If it fails, close the current
support-rule tuning cycle as no focal gap demonstrated/inconclusive, without
claiming impossibility. Stop regardless of outcome; change no defaults/p-values.

Reuse the sealed lifecycle. Independent audits cover full source inventories,
roles/IDs, raw laws/joins, feature whitelist, every tree/stage/leaf and native
float32 conversion, original heads/policies, thresholds/ties/selection, all metrics,
intervals, failure bounds, the stopping flag and plotted values. Guard failures
stop dependent work; retain failed attempts without replacements.

At most two single-thread workers; ten serial preflight minutes and a measured
projection below thirty serial main/audit/report minutes, including 25% allowance
and no parallel-speed credit. Cap new retained scientific and engineering evidence
at three GiB; count staging twice, add 25% scientific storage allowance and
128 MiB reserve, and require eight GiB free. Tooling/CI/engineering time is reported
separately. Verified lossless replacement of exact task-owned working copies needs
complete round-trip manifests and never removes earlier research.

Publish only code/docs/invented fixtures/figures/small aggregates, each changed
file below one MiB and total changed content below eight MiB. Keep full evidence
and CI logs local. Test Python 3.11/3.12, package allowlists and installed workflows;
push directly to master, verify all six exact-head CI jobs, close preservation
and resource receipts, then stop.
