# Class-local support validation protocol

The frozen candidate replaces a class center's support boundary with distance to
its fifth original training neighbor, preserving the pooled metric, classifiers,
.05 rank calibration and decision semantics. There is no neighbor/metric/threshold
search and no change to the original joint target.

Assess eighty source-bound archived observations without classifier retraining.
Eight representative archival observations belong to the separate measured
preflight, seventy-two to main; they enter the archival report once. Freeze all
source/settings/seeds before archived scoring or fresh scientific generation.

Fresh validation has eighty independent training datasets: ten per weak-linear,
standard-linear, allometry+saturation and confounded-size law at N=64/256. A
separate eight-fresh-dataset preflight is excluded from performance estimates.
Retain all three residual seeds (0/101/202) and unchanged logistic regression.
Use independent known calibration (160), known IID evaluation (2,048), nearby/far/
indistinguishable unfamiliar groups (512 each), and shifted-known evaluation
(2,048) for confounded size. Complete role identities/innovations are disjoint.
Unknown labels enter metrics only; illustrative groups are not species truth.

The same predictions receive unscreened, probability, pooled-distance and local
support decisions. Main trains 320 classifiers; preflight trains 32. Across phases,
264 neural trainings have all 132,000 actual optimizer updates independently
checked; eighty-eight fits are logistic. There are 320 archival fixed-model
assessments, eight exact frozen prediction/policy replays and 11,424 external
model/policy/cohort metric records. No GMM fits, null/stability draws or empirical
analysis are included.

The known-generating-law reference uses true five-dimensional latent/size laws,
not observed class identities as predictors. Its known/unseen likelihood-ratio
sweep has nineteen fixed calibration quantiles and 13,566 point/cohort evaluations.
It uses unseen-group laws unavailable in applications; it is not a deployment
method, formal recovery bound or threshold-selection tool.

Retain the absolute target: >=80% IID known acceptance, >=90% accepted accuracy
and <=10% acceptance of BOTH near and far unfamiliar groups. Forty-eight primary
joint-target rates cover eight cells, two primary classifiers and three screens.
Sixteen further benefit-indicator rates compare local with distance support:
>=10 percentage-point coverage gain, >=90% accepted accuracy, <=1 percentage-point
accuracy loss and <=2 percentage-point increases for either unfamiliar group.
This descriptive indicator is not noninferiority or adequate unfamiliar detection.
All sixty-four rates receive exact marginal and separate Bonferroni-64 simultaneous
intervals and failure bounds. Thirty-two paired comparisons retain discordant
outcomes and descriptive continuous changes. Ten datasets/cell support a pilot.
Datasets are independent units; seeds, policies, cohorts and diagnostic thresholds
are repeated measurements. Empty accepted accuracies remain undefined.

Keep shifted and observationally overlapping controls separate from known-class
inclusion assumptions; identical measured laws cannot reveal a new identity.
Retain all failures without replacements. Independently audit laws/joins, old
predictions, every native neural update, whitening, direct quadratic distances,
neighbor order/ties, ranks, decisions, analytical density sweeps, metrics and
uncertainty. No policy/default promotion follows automatically.

The measured full-settings preflight represents every cell/model/policy. Resource
limits: three GiB new retained local scientific/engineering evidence, eight GiB
free, two single-thread workers, twenty serial preflight minutes, ninety serial
main/audit minutes, 25% projection allowance and 128 MiB reserve; staging is
counted twice. Preserve failed guards and require an amendment to change them.
Publish code/docs/figures/invented examples/small aggregates only. Full specimens,
labels, libraries, weights, scores and logs remain local. No author contact.

The separate `benchmark-local-reference-support` lifecycle provides `bind`,
`freeze`, `run`, `audit`, `gate` and `report`, with an explicitly invented fixture
mode for installation checks. Historical source inventories remain immutable.
