# Amphipod pilot feasibility findings

The frozen raw-morphology policies produce reproducible descriptive rosters,
but do not establish an independently validated species-delimitation benchmark.
All 1,123 original rows were assessed under each of four fixed policies.

| Panel | Source sex label | Complete raw specimens | Documentary specimen links | Molecular-partition links |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Core 19 | Male | 241 | 5 | 4 |
| Core 19 | Female | 236 | 7 | 6 |
| Somatic 24 | Male | 159 | 0 | 0 |
| Somatic 24 | Female | 169 | 0 | 0 |

The core panel therefore retains 477 distinct source specimens, including ten
with documented molecular-partition links. The 328 specimens in the secondary
panel are subsets of those core rosters, not additional independent observations.
The secondary panel has no documentary specimen links under the existing
reference rules. No missing value was filled and no trait policy was relaxed
after these counts became known. The raw-source ingestion representation and
117 previously documented partition links were not treated as approved cohorts.

The loss of molecular overlap under complete-case rules is a material limitation:
more retained morphological specimens do not automatically give enough linked
reference evidence. Targeted selection, missingness, sex/stage ambiguity,
uncorrected body-size effects and undocumented dependence also remain. Published
PTP/ASAP partitions are hypotheses, not independently adjudicated species truth.
No clustering accuracy, concordance score or species significance was computed.

| Purpose | Outcome |

| Grouped morphology/ecology analysis | No-go: independent groups and continuous ecology not established |
| Bootstrap or other independent-unit resampling | No-go: resampling units not justified |
| Significance calibration | No-go: validated null for this design absent |
| Independent species-boundary validation | No-go: reference truth and sampling independence unestablished |

Opening inference requires a documented event/identity crosswalk and a justified
dependence design. Species-boundary validation additionally requires independent
specimen-linked taxonomic adjudication. Locality/date equality, public barcodes
or a complete numerical matrix cannot supply these assumptions. This checkpoint
ends at that boundary rather than silently certifying a cohort.

The independent audit verified all 4,492 row decisions, raw-cell provenance,
every descriptive roster summary field and the closed inferential gates. Complete
prior assessments were reconstructed from unchanged local sources. All defaults,
old schemas, production fitting/sampling and historical p-values remain unchanged.
No numerical model was fitted, no resampling or significance analysis occurred,
no sources were downloaded, and no author/custodian was contacted. Full evidence
stays local; the public results contain only aggregate counts.
